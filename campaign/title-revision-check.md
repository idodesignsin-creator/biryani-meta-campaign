Section 3: **every top seller leads their title with "Garam Masala," not "Biryani."** Confirms the
finding rather than contradicting it.

## Title: one real gap

**Proposed:** `Spiceto Garam Masala Whole 90g | 12-Spice Khada Mix for Biryani, Kerala` (71 chars)

Leading with "Garam Masala Whole" is correct and matches the data. But **the token `sabut` is
absent entirely.** We hold real click-share data for `sabut garam masala`, `garam masala sabut`
and `khada masala sabut` — genuine, if volatile, search volume with captured conversion figures
(see `brand-analytics-garam-masala.md`). Dropping the token means the title stops matching those
queries.

**Recommended revision — 75/75 chars, full coverage restored:**
```
Spiceto Garam Masala Whole 90g | Khada Sabut Mix, 12 Spice, Biryani, Kerala
```

Covers every search term this project holds real data for except `biryani whole spices` /
`biryani spices whole` (missing only the word "spices") — already carried by the bullets and
backend keywords.

## Bullets: one repeat, two new unverified claims

**Bullet 2 revives "99% consistency."** Already flagged in `listing-fixes.md` §6 as an unsourced
statistic that invites doubt rather than settling it — the other session didn't have that
context. Use `"identical ratio in every pack"` instead; same claim, no invented number.

**Bullet 5 adds "Premium export-grade" and "100% vegan."** Neither appears in the verified facts
table in `ad-copy.md`. Whole spices are very likely vegan incidentally, and Spiceto may genuinely
be export-grade — but neither has been confirmed as an existing, stated fact. Keep only if
verified true; otherwise these are two new unforced compliance risks.

**Bullet 3 names Thalassery, Kozhikode and Hyderabadi biryani specifically.** Verified provenance
is "developed with biryani experts in Kerala" — that supports Thalassery/Kozhikode. Hyderabadi
biryani is a distinct regional tradition with a different spice profile; claiming the ratio is
*tested for* it specifically goes beyond what's been verified. Consider softening to the general
Kerala-expert claim already substantiated elsewhere.

## Item Highlight — no issues found

`50+ servings | Hand-weighed 12 spices for consistency | Zero additives, Idukki-grown` (86 chars)
— clean, no unverified claims, no overlap with the revised title's tokens.

## Net recommendation

Adopt the "garam masala leads" title strategy — it's correct and now confirmed by two
independent analyses of the same competitor set. Use the 75-char revision above to keep the
`sabut` token. Fix bullet 2's "99% consistency" the same way already applied elsewhere in this
project. Confirm "export-grade" and "vegan" before keeping them, and soften the Hyderabadi
claim in bullet 3.
