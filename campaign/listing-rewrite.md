## Read this first: keywords alone will not win organic rank

Amazon organic position is driven by, in order:

1. **Sales velocity and conversion rate** — dominant
2. **Relevance** — keywords in title, bullets, backend, item type
3. **Reviews and rating**

The listing currently ranks for almost nothing because the title omits every high-volume term.
Fixing that makes the listing **eligible** to rank. It does not by itself create rank — that
needs sales velocity, which needs the reviews Vine is meant to supply.

So: do this rewrite **and** the Vine work. The rewrite alone will move impressions, not orders.
