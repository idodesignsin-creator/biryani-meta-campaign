# SEO Comparison — Crystal Kuruva vs Mountain Shadows

Competitive SEO review of [crystalkuruva.com](https://www.crystalkuruva.com/) against
[mountainshadows.in](https://www.mountainshadows.in/), both luxury resorts in Wayanad,
Kerala.

## ⚠️ Brand name — we've been spelling it wrong

The resort's own domain and site say **"Crystal Kuruva"** (Crystal Kuruva Nature Resort &
Spa), not "Cristal Kuruva". The audience doc in this folder and the folder name itself
use the misspelling. Worth correcting before anything ships to the client — and before it
ends up in ad copy, where a misspelt brand name loses brand-search traffic outright.

## ⚠️ How this was researched — read before trusting it

This sandbox's network policy blocks both domains (403 at the proxy), so **neither site
was crawled directly.** Everything below is derived from Google's search index — page
titles and URLs are real and current, but the following were **not verifiable** and need
checking by someone with browser access:

- meta descriptions, H1/H2 structure, body word counts
- schema markup (Hotel / LocalBusiness / FAQ), canonical tags, hreflang
- image alt text, page speed, Core Web Vitals, mobile rendering
- internal link structure and anchor text
- backlink profiles and domain authority for either site

Treat the structural findings as solid and the on-page findings as directional.

## The single biggest gap: commercial landing pages

**Mountain Shadows builds keyword-targeted landing pages. Crystal Kuruva doesn't.**

Look at the URL slugs. Mountain Shadows has dedicated, keyword-in-URL pages for its
highest-intent commercial searches:

- `private-pool-villa-resort-wayanad-kerala.php`
- `staycation-resorts-kerala.php`
- `ayurveda-and-wellness.php`

Crystal Kuruva collapses its entire room inventory — Deluxe Cottage, Premium Cottage,
Family Twin Cottage, Premium Family Duplex, Premium Villa with Tub, Tree House, Private
Pool Villa — into a single generic `/cottages.php`.

The consequence is direct and measurable: **Crystal Kuruva's homepage title bids for
"Pool Villa in Wayanad," but the site has no pool villa page.** It is trying to win a
high-intent commercial keyword with a homepage, against a competitor that built a
purpose-made page for exactly that term. That is a losing matchup regardless of how good
the property is.

## Where each site has a genuine moat — don't fight the wrong battle

The two resorts sit in **different parts of Wayanad**, and each owns a distinct
geographic keyword set:

- **Mountain Shadows** — Padinjarathara / Kuttiyamvayal, overlooking **Banasura Sagar
  lake**. Owns "lake view resort Wayanad", "resorts near Banasura Sagar", "infinity pool
  Wayanad".
- **Crystal Kuruva** — Pakkom, Mananthavady–Pulpally Road, beside **Kuruva Island
  (Kuruvadweep)**, a 950-acre protected river delta on the Kabini tributaries.

**Recommendation: Crystal Kuruva should not chase "lake view" terms.** It's a weaker
claim geographically and the competitor has a dam-view property to back theirs. Instead,
own the Kuruva Island cluster completely — "resorts near Kuruva Island", "Kuruvadweep
stay", "Pulpally resorts", "Mananthavady resorts", "resorts near Kabini river". These are
lower-volume but far lower-competition, and they carry the highest possible relevance for
a property literally named after the island.

Crystal Kuruva already blogs about Kuruva Island in two posts. That's informational
coverage. It needs a **commercial** page for those terms too.

## Crystal Kuruva's clear advantage: content

Crystal Kuruva runs an active blog — **at least 8 indexed posts** on clean, descriptive
URL slugs targeting real informational queries (romantic escapes, forest stay, nature
walks, Kuruva Island). **Mountain Shadows has no blog indexed at all.**

This is Crystal Kuruva's strongest existing asset and the one area where it's clearly
ahead. Two things to do with it:

1. **Internally link every blog post to the relevant commercial page.** Blog traffic is
   informational — it doesn't book. Its value is passing authority and readers to the
   money pages. A post about romantic escapes should link to a honeymoon/pool-villa page;
   a Kuruva Island post should link to `/resorts-near-kuruva-island`. Right now those
   destination pages don't exist, which means the blog's authority has nowhere to flow.
2. **Keep publishing.** The competitor isn't contesting this channel at all.

## Technical issues to fix on Crystal Kuruva

Ordered by effort-to-impact:

1. **Broken title tag.** `/blogs/explore-the-natural-charm-of-kuruva-island` has the title
   `Crystalkuruva` — a default fallback with zero keywords, on a post about the resort's
   single most valuable geographic asset. Fix immediately.
2. **Duplicate content across two URL systems.** `/blog-details.php?id=7` and
   `/blogs/{slug}` both serve blog content and both are indexed. This is a partial
   migration that never finished. 301-redirect every `blog-details.php?id=` URL to its
   clean-slug equivalent and set canonical tags.
3. **Inconsistent host.** Both `crystalkuruva.com` and `www.crystalkuruva.com` appear in
   the index. Pick one, 301 the other, and confirm the canonical host in Search Console.
4. **Homepage title carries no brand name.** "Luxury Resorts in Wayanad | Pool Villa in
   Wayanad" — brand searches are the highest-converting queries a resort gets, and the
   homepage should compete for them. Suggested rewrite:
   `Crystal Kuruva | Luxury Resort & Pool Villas near Kuruva Island, Wayanad` (67 chars).
5. **Mixed URL conventions.** Main pages are `.php`, blog is clean-slug. New pages should
   all use clean slugs; leave existing `.php` pages alone unless redirecting them properly.

## What Mountain Shadows does badly — don't copy it

Worth naming so these aren't mistaken for best practice:

- **Homepage title is keyword-stuffed and sloppy:** "Best 5 Star Luxury Resort Hotel in
  Wayanad |Top Resorts in WAYANAD" — repeats the geo twice, shouts it in caps, missing a
  space after the pipe, and includes no brand name. Reads as spam to a human scanning
  results, which costs click-through even where it ranks.
- **`/gallery.php` title is "THE EXCEPTIONAL PART Explore Gallery"** — no brand, no geo,
  no keyword. Looks like unedited design placeholder text shipped to production.
- **`/experiences.php` title has no geo qualifier** at all.

Crystal Kuruva's titles are, on the whole, **better written than the competitor's**. The
gap isn't title quality — it's that there aren't enough pages.

## Recommended order of work

1. Fix the broken blog title and the duplicate-URL/canonical issues (days, not weeks).
2. Rewrite the homepage title to include the brand.
3. Build `/pool-villa-resort-wayanad` and `/treehouse-resort-wayanad` — the two highest-value
   gaps, one contested and one uncontested.
4. Build `/resorts-near-kuruva-island` and internally link both Kuruva Island blog posts
   into it.
5. Split `/cottages.php` into per-room-type pages, or at minimum add family and honeymoon
   landing pages.
6. Run the checks this review couldn't: schema markup, Core Web Vitals, mobile, backlinks.
