# Launch Checklist

## Before you spend a rupee — audit the listing

Meta ads amplify whatever your Amazon listing already does. If the listing converts at
2%, ads make you lose money faster. Spend an hour here before spending ₹500/day.

Open `amazon.in/dp/B0H6TP1DNS` on a phone, logged out, and check:

- [ ] **Main image** — pure white background, product fills ~85% of frame, no text or
      badges burned in (Amazon's rule, and it gets listings suppressed)
- [ ] **6+ images** including: whole spices laid out, the grinding step, a finished
      biryani, an infographic naming the 12+ spices, a scale/size reference
- [ ] **Title** carries the search terms — biryani, whole spices, khada garam masala,
      the weight
- [ ] **A+ Content** live (requires Brand Registry). This is a real conversion lever on a
      premium-priced spice, where the buyer needs a reason to pay above supermarket price.
- [ ] **Bullets** lead with the whole-vs-ground argument, not with generic spice adjectives
- [ ] **Price and pack size** visible and competitive against ground masala alternatives
- [ ] **In stock**, with a delivery promise inside a week to all 12 target cities
- [ ] **At least a handful of reviews.** Under ~10 reviews, cold traffic converts poorly
      no matter how good the ad is. If you're below that, consider running the Vine
      programme or a small Sponsored Products budget to seed reviews first.
- [ ] **Brand Registry active** — gates A+ Content and Amazon Attribution both

Honest guidance: if the listing has fewer than 10 reviews and no A+ Content, fixing those
two things will return more than the first ₹15,000 of Meta spend will.

## Campaign build

- [ ] Campaign created: `SPICETO | Traffic | Prospecting | IN-Metro`, Traffic objective
- [ ] CBO on, ₹500/day
- [ ] Ad Set A: 12 cities + radii, 25–54, **All genders**, interest stack narrowed by
      shopping behaviour, optimisation = **Landing page views**, Advantage+ placements
- [ ] Ad Set B: same 12 cities, 25–54, All genders, no detailed targeting,
      Advantage+ Audience on
- [ ] Both ad sets: "People living in this location" (not "recently in")
- [ ] 3 ads per ad set, each with its own Attribution URL
- [ ] CTA button = `Shop Now` on all six ads
- [ ] Every destination URL clicked and verified
- [ ] Everything left **PAUSED**

## Final review before unpausing

- [ ] Preview every ad on mobile feed, Reels and Stories in Ads Manager
- [ ] Read every line of copy once more against the compliance table
- [ ] Confirm estimated daily reach is non-trivial in both ad sets — a very low estimate
      means targeting is over-constrained
- [ ] Confirm the total daily budget is what you intend and the schedule has no end date
      you didn't set

Then unpause and don't touch it for 72 hours.

## First 14 days

| Day | Do | Don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Unpause. Walk away. | Don't watch it hourly. |
| 1–2 | Check once daily: is it delivering, any rejections? | **Don't optimise.** Learning phase. Early data is noise. |
| 3 | Confirm spend is pacing and all six ads are delivering. Fix anything blocked. | Don't pause ads on 3-day CTR. |
| 7 | Full review: CPLPV by ad, by ad set, **by gender**, **by age**, by city. Pause clearly dead ads. **Make the gender call.** | Don't change budget and targeting in the same edit. |
| 8–13 | Let the changes settle. Start producing round-2 creative. | Don't keep editing. Every edit restarts learning. |
| 14 | Meta data + first real Attribution numbers. Cut bottom cities. Pick the winning angle. Decide scale / restructure / stop. | Don't scale on Meta metrics alone — wait for the Amazon numbers. |

## Kill criteria

Decide these now, while you're unemotional about it. Stop or restructure if, at day 14:

- CTR is under 0.4% across all creative → the creative is the problem, not the targeting
- CPLPV is above ₹25 with no city or audience performing → the audience thesis is wrong
- Attribution shows detail page views but an add-to-cart rate under 5% → the listing is
  the problem; stop spending and fix it
- CPA is more than 3× your break-even and no lever is closing the gap

None of these mean the product is wrong. They tell you which of the three things —
creative, targeting, listing — to go fix before spending more.
