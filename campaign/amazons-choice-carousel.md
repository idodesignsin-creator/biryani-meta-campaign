# Carousel — "Your kitchen chose us. Now Amazon did too."

An 8-slide Instagram / Facebook carousel announcing that four Spiceto products earned
Amazon's Choice on Amazon.in. It builds on the poster in `amazons-choice-poster.md`, which
becomes slide 1.

**Format:** 4:5, 1080 × 1350 px per slide. That's the tallest size the feed allows, and
every slide must be the same size.

**Placeholders:** `[PRODUCT 2]`, `[PRODUCT 3]` and `[PRODUCT 4]` need real names, pack sizes,
prices and one real fact each. Only the Biryani Whole Spices details are confirmed in this
repo. Don't invent claims to fill the gaps.

## How the slides connect

Run **a trail of whole spices** along the bottom of every slide (cardamom, cinnamon, star
anise, pepper) and let it cross the edge between slides. The spice cut off on the right of
slide 3 continues on the left of slide 4. This makes people want to swipe, and ties eight
separate frames into one piece.

- **Background:** deep maroon throughout. Slide 7 is the exception (see below).
- **Type:** cream serif for headlines, gold for the key word or number, and a clean sans for
  body text.
- **Slide counter:** a small gold "1/8, 2/8…" in the top-right corner.
- **Badge tag:** the same gold ribbon reading "Amazon's Choice" on every product. Set it in
  your own type. Never use Amazon's badge artwork or logo.

## Slide 7 — Where it comes from

**Visual:** switch to a real photo from Idukki: spice plantations, the hills, or hands weighing spices
on a scale. This is the one slide without a maroon background. The change of pace keeps
people swiping.

> **FROM IDUKKI, KERALA**
>
> Every pack is weighed by hand, in small batches, by the people who grow up with these
> spices.
>
> That's what your kitchen noticed first.

*Only keep the "small batches" and "grow up with these spices" lines if they're true. If
they aren't, cut them and keep "Every pack is weighed by hand."*
