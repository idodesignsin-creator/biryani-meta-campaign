# Amazon Brand Analytics — Top Search Terms (2026-08-10)

Source: Seller Central → Brand Analytics → Search Analytics → Top Search Terms. Unlocked by
Brand Registry approval. **This is market-wide Amazon data**, not account-specific ad
performance — the strongest competitive intelligence gathered in this project so far.

`Search Frequency Rank`: position among *all* Amazon.in search terms. **Lower number = more
searched.**

## 1. The "biryani" category is not owned by whole-spice blends

Top 20 terms under `biryani`, July 2026 (monthly):

| Category | Count in top 20 |
| --- | --- |
| Ground masala brands (Shan, Everest, Abida Rasheed, Daawat) | 6 |
| Cookware (pots, handi) | 5 |
| Rice (basmati) | 3 |
| Ready-to-cook mixes | 2 |
| Ready-to-eat meals | 2 |
| Biryani essence (flavour concentrate — different product) | 1 |
| Fried onions (adjacent ingredient) | 1 |

**None of the top 20 searches are for a whole-spice blend to grind at home.** Spiceto occupies a
genuine sub-niche within "biryani," not the category's mainstream.

## 2. Independent confirmation of low search volume

market-wide index, not paid-campaign data.

## 3. The critical finding: this category converts — for one listing

Week 29 (12–18 Jul 2026), by search term and top-clicked product:

| Search term | Product | Click share | **Conversion share** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| biryani masala whole spices | Manasa Spices 10g | 18.42% | 0% |
| biryani masala whole spices | MAI RASOI Whole Sabut Mix (50g) | 26.42% | **0%** |
| biryani spices whole | MAI RASOI Ananas Flower/Star Anise (single spice) | 11.43% | 0% |
| **biryani spices whole** | **JAIPUR MASALE Khada Garam Masala \| Whole Spices Blend \| Sabut…** | 8.82% | **33.33%** |

**One listing, on the same search terms as three others, converts at 33% while the rest show
0%.** JAIPUR MASALE's positioning — khada garam masala, whole spices blend, sabut — is close to
Spiceto's own. This is direct evidence that **the category is not inherently hard to sell**; a
specific listing is doing something right that the others are not.
