# Poster concept — four Spiceto products earn Amazon's Choice

A celebration poster for Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp Status: four Spiceto products
now carry the Amazon's Choice badge on Amazon.in.

**Still needed:** names, pack sizes and prices of the other three products. This repo only
documents the Biryani Whole Spices (Khada Garam Masala, ASIN `B0H6TP1DNS`). Slots 2–4 below
are placeholders until those details arrive.

## The idea — "Your kitchen chose us. Now Amazon did too."

**Chosen headline (2026-09-24).** The badge comes second in this line. Customers come
first, and Amazon's recognition follows them. That keeps the poster warm and grateful
rather than boastful, and it credits the people who actually earned the badges.

- **Headline:** **YOUR KITCHEN CHOSE US.** / **NOW AMAZON DID TOO.**
  - Line 1 in cream, line 2 in gold, so the eye lands on the payoff.
- **Sub-headline:** Four Spiceto products are now *Amazon's Choice* on Amazon.in
- **Proof line:** Hand-weighed in Idukki, Kerala · From our kitchen to yours
- **CTA:** Search "Spiceto" on Amazon

- **Hero:** the four packs standing together like a winners' podium. The Biryani pouch
  goes in the centre-left, slightly forward, because it has the proof: 5.0 stars and
  the Moosakka story.
- **Tag on each pack:** a small gold ribbon reading **"Amazon's Choice"** in your own
  type. Don't use Amazon's badge artwork (see compliance below).
- **Foreground:** a few whole spices from each product (cardamom, cinnamon, star anise,
  pepper) scattered across the wood. This keeps the food-in-frame lesson from Round 2.
- **Palette:** deep maroon or kerala-green background, gold accents, cream type. Warm, like
  the shipped Round-3 creative, so the set reads as one brand.

## 9:16 Story / Reel / WhatsApp Status (1080 × 1920)

Rebuild the layout for this size instead of cropping it (see `creative-brief.md`):

- Headline in the upper-middle, below the 250 px top safe zone.
- Packs in a **2 × 2 grid**, not a row. Four packs across 1080 px get too small to read.
- CTA and QR above the 400 px bottom safe zone.
- **Motion option (6–8 s):** open on a home kitchen, a hand reaching for a Spiceto pack,
  with "YOUR KITCHEN CHOSE US." Cut to the four packs as the badges appear one at a time,
  1 → 2 → 3 → 4, and "NOW AMAZON DID TOO." lands. The two halves of the line map onto the
  two shots, and it works with sound off.

## Compliance — check before posting

| Risk | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Amazon's badge artwork and logo** | Amazon's trademark guidelines don't allow its logos or badge graphics in your own marketing without permission. Say "Amazon's Choice" in your own type. Don't copy the dark-blue badge or the smile logo. This matches the "no Amazon branding" rule in `creative-brief.md`. |
| **The badge isn't permanent** | Amazon adds and removes Amazon's Choice automatically, based on search keyword, price and availability. Before posting, check all four listings still show it, and screenshot each one as proof. Don't use this poster in paid ads for long. If a badge drops, the claim becomes false. |
| **It's tied to a search keyword** | The badge is given for a specific search (for example, "biryani masala whole"). Say "Amazon's Choice", not "Amazon's #1" or "Best on Amazon". |
| **Spelling** | BIRYANI, not BIRIYANI. WEIGHED, not WELGHED. Both errors have already shipped once. |
| **No health claims** | No "100% natural" or wellness icons (same policy as the ad copy). |
